## Scope

This page is a **cross-directory navigation hub**, not a move/rename of
historical files and not another active-status dashboard. It preserves stable
URLs, old CI/commit proof and the meaning of earlier proposals. Later accepted
work can supersede a plan without invalidating what that plan recorded.

## Protection rules

Do not delete ADR decisions, real-runtime acceptance, rollback/provenance
evidence, historical phase plans or complete phase indexes just because they
have repeated headings or outdated 'current' wording. A file can be valuable
because it records the specific **time and scope** of a decision. For
the deletion matrix, refer to the [Pruning Review](../development/documentation-pruning-review.md).
